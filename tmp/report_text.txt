3.1 Festive Market Reality: Size, Timing & Growth Drivers
Festive commerce in India is not a Diwali-week spike. Interest and spend build early, and growth is coming from structural shifts — not only discounts.
Market scale: Festive Gross Merchandise Value context of about Rs 1.2 Lakh Crore (2025).
Velocity: The first 11 days of the 2025 sales event alone crossed Rs 60,000+ Crore GMV.
Timing: Search interest for 'Diwali Sale' starts as early as August — planning must begin then, not in October.
Growth (~27% festive shopping growth) came from three forces: Tier 2+ city demand, higher spend per buyer, and quick commerce.
Tier 2+ / Bharat: 60–65% of shoppers; roughly half of incremental e-retail orders.
Spend: About 70% of buyers spent more than in 2024 (GST cuts + early platform access).
Quick commerce: Net order value more than doubled YoY; festive share rose from 8% (2024) to 12%.
Who shops: Gen Z is 40–45% of the e-retail shopper base — a core cohort, not a niche.
Learning: Festive strategy must prioritise Bharat + Gen Z, start in August, and treat quick commerce as a mainstream festive channel.
3.2 Shopper Journey: Research-Heavy, Not Impulse-Only
The strongest behavioural lesson: festive buying is a multi-touch journey. Discovery may start on social, but validation and immersion happen on Google and YouTube.
9 in 10 shoppers research before purchasing; 33% do 'a lot of research'.
Average journey intensity: ~8.3 search-engine searches and ~7.7 online video views.
Immersive stage: After inspiration, shoppers go deep — checking whether a product fits their identity and physical space. It is not a 'quick click'.
Social → Search handoff: 7 in 10 festive shoppers who saw brands on Facebook/Instagram went to Google Search to research further.
Omnichannel reality: Shoppers make ~6.6 physical store visits on average; 84% check digital sources before visiting a store.
Speed closes the sale: 81% choose where to buy based on delivery speed; 90% want reliable shipping in two days or less.
Nearly 30% used quick commerce for festive shopping — it has become a staple final step of the journey.
Learning: Winning festive demand means owning research moments (Search/YouTube), connecting digital to store, and promising (and delivering) speed.
3.3 AI Is Now Shopping Infrastructure
The biggest mindset shift from the sessions: shoppers will no longer only 'try' AI — they will rely on it, especially for complex categories.
AI Overview: ~2.5 billion monthly users.
AI Mode: 1 billion+ monthly users (Gemini 1.5 era capabilities highlighted).
95% of consumers using AI Mode for shopping find it helpful.
AI acts as a personal shopper: comparing specs, clarifying needs, simplifying jargon, shortening decision time, and answering nuanced questions (~38–40% across roles).
Highest AI chatbot dependency: Mobile devices (89%), large home appliances (87%), furniture (84%), consumer electronics (83%). Apparel is lower but still material (69%).
India leads globally in AI marketing expectations: 53% of Indian CMOs expect 5–9% incremental growth from AI in marketing; 27% rank AI adoption in their top-3 priorities.
Retail AI impact (Google Cloud framing): >$3T estimated agentic commerce revenue by 2030; 81% of consumers expect faster service due to AI; ~24% reduction in frontline burnout when AI is used.
Data foundation for AI retail: Cleanliness (accurate data), Connectivity (open to agents), Context (actionable enrichment).
Learning: For any product or brand, AI-ready content (clear specs, comparisons, FAQs, structured feeds) is now as important as creative ads.
3.4 Media Systems That Matter This Festive Season
YouTube Shorts & Creator Partnerships
Short-form video is a primary catalyst for impulse purchases: 61% of shoppers who bought quickly (with little research) used YouTube Shorts in their journey.
Creator Partnerships positioning: 'Capture Hearts (and Carts)' — emotional trust plus commerce.
Repeatable flow: Connect with creators → create brand videos → boost as Partnership Ads → measure in Creator Partnership Hub.
YouTube Prime Time Masthead (IPL example) was presented as a high-impact 'first eyeball' format.
Connected TV (CTV)
Google positioned as the CTV destination for the festive season.
YouTube: #1 streaming platform in India.
Google TV: #1 TV base operating system in India.
DV360: access to ~86% reach of CTV households in India.
Local / location advantage
In an AI-first world, location is a competitive advantage.
Three-step local framework: Be Visible (Business Profile + AI Search) → Build Connections (local conversion goals / Local Campaigns) → Drive Performance (connect and activate data signals).
Ride-hailing inventory
Premium high-intent placements on apps like Uber and Rapido (On Trip / Journey Ads; native & display; location-based shopping-hub formats).
Framed opportunity: be seen during high-intent commute moments.
Commerce Media Suite & Performance Max
Commerce Media Suite now spans multiple campaign types: Performance Max, Video, Demand Gen, Search, and Shopping.
Steer AI — do not set and forget: channel steering, demographic exclusions, first-party exclusions (e.g. returners), and product-value bid adjustments.
Measurement & privacy
iOS performance stability can be recovered with on-device conversion measurement — a privacy-preserving way to restore signal for models.
Media planning (DV360 / Canvas)
Media planning can be visualised geographically (Brand GRPs, Social GRPs, investment by city/state).
Festive next steps taught: (1) consolidate media buys on DV360 across the journey, (2) activate programmatic DOOH where people gather, (3) align briefs with Google/DV360 POCs.
Learning: Festive media is a system — Search + YouTube + CTV + Local + Creators + measurement — not isolated channel spends.

4. Personal Takeaways — Mujhe Specifically Kya Mila
Ye section event ke after-reflection se aata hai: jo cheezein ab practical soch / kaam mein seedha apply ho sakti hain.
1. Festive planning starts in August
Diwali interest builds early. Waiting for sale week means missing the research window when shoppers are already immersing on Google and YouTube.
2. Bharat + Gen Z are the dual growth engine
Tier 2+ cities and Gen Z together define volume and culture. Ignoring either means leaving the larger festive opportunity untouched.
3. Social discovers; Google decides
Even after Instagram/Facebook discovery, most festive shoppers validate on Google Search. SEO, Shopping feed quality, AI Overview readiness, and YouTube education content matter more than vanity social reach.
4. AI is shopping infrastructure, not a novelty
For complex categories, AI already behaves like a personal shopper. Product data, clear specs, and comparison content are the new storefront UX.
5. Speed is a marketing message
With quick commerce rising and two-day shipping expectations dominant, delivery reliability is part of brand promise — not only an operations KPI.
6. Creators need a measurement loop
Creator work should move from one-off posts to a system: create → boost as Partnership Ads → measure impact. Hearts without carts is incomplete.
7. Steer AI; do not only automate
PMax and Commerce Media Suite work best when humans guide them with channel controls, exclusions, and product-value signals.
8. Measurement recovery is a competitive skill
Privacy changes (especially iOS) reduce signal. On-device conversion measurement and clean first-party data are growth capabilities, not backend chores.
9. Omnichannel must be designed, not assumed
Digital research, store visits, CTV living-room presence, and festive DOOH all connect. Channel-silo campaigns underperform a journey-based plan.
10. Culture and commerce are linked at Google
The event itself showed how Google connects cultural moments (festivals) with product systems (ads, cloud, retail media) — a useful model for product storytelling.

6. Closing Summary
Khushiyon Ka Tyohaar was a compressed masterclass on how India shops in 2025–26: research-heavy, AI-assisted, omnichannel, speed-obsessed, and creator-influenced. The clearest overall lesson is that festive winners prepare early, respect Bharat and Gen Z, feed AI good data, and show up as a connected system across Search, YouTube, CTV, local, and retail media — not as scattered campaigns.
